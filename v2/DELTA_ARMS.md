# Phoenix V2 Delta Profile paper arms

Arms C and D are independent long-only experiments on the configured Bybit USDT perpetuals. Each starts with 1,000 virtual USDT. The RC3F-1 A/B strategy, policy hash, books, and existing journal rows retain their original state. C/D use the same read-only data connection and write their own arm-tagged events to the durable journal.

## Data and timestamps

- Use `publicTrade.{symbol}` from Bybit's linear WebSocket. Each row must have a unique trade ID within its minute, a native price and quantity, an exchange fill timestamp, an observed receipt time, and a taker side of `Buy` or `Sell`. The existing worker reconciles every minute's observed volume and OHLC against the exchange candle. The candle validates coverage and supplies ordinary ATR and mark inputs. Every signed volume bin comes from the trades themselves.
- The archive probe on 2026-11-11 returned HTTP 200 and read the gzip CSV header and first trade from the 2026-11-10 daily files for all 12 deployed symbols. The files carry timestamp, symbol, side, size, price, and match ID. All 527 archived SOLUSDT prints at 17:08 UTC on 2026-11-10 matched the deployed worker's capture by ID, side, price, size and timestamp at millisecond precision. The files support historical footprint studies after coverage checks. They do not carry local live receipt times. This version builds its profiles prospectively from the worker's reconciled capture. Recent Trades is bounded to 1,000 rows for linear contracts and cannot establish a full hour or fill arbitrary gaps.
- Retain a completed UTC hour with at least 100 prints as the reference for the next hour. Use bins of `ceil_to_tick(0.001 × first price)` with a one-tick minimum. Use the lowest price in a tie at the first exchange millisecond. Bins are aligned to zero. Taker buys add to buy volume and taker sells add to sell volume. The point of control is the bin with maximum total volume, with ties going to the lower bin. Expand to adjacent bins until at least 70% of total volume is inside, choosing the larger next bin and then the lower bin on a tie.
- Use complete, aligned five-minute footprints with at least 30 prints, against the prior hour's bin width. Each footprint closes at its last observed execution price. A tied closing millisecond can use the independently confirmed native close if it matches a trade in the tied group and the minute passes reconciliation. Otherwise the footprint and its predecessor chain are invalid. Valid, contiguous five-minute summaries carry across UTC hours. Feed gaps and failed reconciliation cancel pending entries and clear the affected footprint and predecessor chain. A verified prior-hour profile remains usable within its original next-hour window if the gap is outside the reference hour. A position exposed to an unknown execution path is retained and marked unresolved. The dashboard displays `DATA UNAVAILABLE` with the reason. Rebuilding a profile needs a new complete hour.

Bybit references: [public trade stream](https://bybit-exchange.github.io/docs/v5/websocket/public/trade), [recent public trades](https://bybit-exchange.github.io/docs/v5/market/recent-trade), [linear trade archives](https://public.bybit.com/trading/BTCUSDT/).

## Arm C: Value Area rejection and reclaim

Select the most negative prior-hour bin strictly below the Value Area Low that actually satisfies the rejection and reclaim rules below. A qualifying bin has at least three prints and net sell volume of at least 40% of its own total volume and 1% of the hour's total volume. Rank by net sell volume, with ties going to the lower price.

Require the preceding five-minute close at or above the bin's upper edge. The current five-minute footprint must trade below that edge, reach no more than one bin width beneath the lower edge, and close back at or above the upper edge and below the prior-hour point of control. After the actual decision, wait for an eligible print through the next five-minute boundary. Cancel if the observed path trades through the structural stop or reaches the selected target before entry. Place the paper stop below the rejection low by the greater of one tick or one-quarter of the bin width. The default target is the point-of-control bin midpoint, rounded down to a valid tick. When the reclaim closes back inside value and the point of control fails 1.5R at the decision reference, use the highest tradable tick within the prior Value Area only if it clears 1.5R at that reference. The target is fixed before the entry attempt.

## Arm D: Seller absorption and breakout

Take resistance from the highest traded price across the preceding three five-minute footprints. Require positive delta of at least 20% of total traded volume in the immediately preceding footprint. The current footprint must reach within one bin width of resistance and close in the top quarter of its range. Require a negative bin in the upper half of that footprint, within one bin width of resistance, at or below the closing price. Require at least three prints in the bin and net selling of at least 40% of its volume and 1% of the footprint's volume. Rank qualifying bins as in C.

After the close and actual decision, arm a paper buy stop one tick above the highest of resistance, the absorption high, and the seller bin's upper edge. Fresh qualifying prints can be evaluated until expiry. Prices beyond the chase cap defer the entry while the setup remains valid. A prior breach of the stop invalidates the intent. The intent expires at the next five-minute boundary. Place the stop below the absorption low by the greater of one tick or one-quarter bin width. Set the target to the tick-rounded price required for at least 2R net of estimated entry and target fees, where R includes estimated stop slippage and fees. This is an experimental mechanical target.

## Execution and account risk

- Evaluate on closed data. Require completion of the decision in the first 90 seconds after the footprint closes. A fill must use an exchange event strictly after that decision and a receipt lag of no more than two seconds. Protection evidence cannot have arrived before entry evidence. An entry at an equal-price timestamp uses the smallest observed fresh print for its quantity cap. Groups with differing prices defer entry. Entry and protective exit quantities stay capped at 1% of their supporting print, rounded down to the native quantity step. An undersized print or a failed reward check can defer the attempt within the original expiry. Equal-price same-millisecond protection fills use stable trade-ID order. Differing actionable prices at the same timestamp quarantine the open liability.
- At entry, reject a reference through the structural stop or a raw market price beyond the reclaim close or breakout trigger by more than the greater of one tick or 0.25 five-minute ATR. The modeled fill price still includes slippage and fees in reward and sizing. Reject less than 1.5R after costs, expired contract or liquidity rules, and quantities below the exchange minimum. Size up to 0.5% of equity by estimated stop loss including fees and slippage, remaining daily loss headroom, and 1× equity notional including the entry fee. One pending intent or one position per arm is allowed. Concurrent qualifying symbols are considered in symbol order.
- Price market entries and stop/time exits adversely by the greater of 5 bps or 0.05 five-minute ATR plus tick rounding. Targets require a public print one tick through the target, then model a fill at the target. Use the worker's experimental taker fee for every fill. Book evidenced funding at the existing mark convention. Missing funding or ambiguous protection evidence locks the affected arm and leaves the position unresolved. While quantity is unresolved, funding is logged as unbooked and the cash ledger stays intact. A position that has neither stopped nor reached its target enters time-exit mode after two hours.
- Compare marked equity to the arm's UTC day-start equity. Lock new entries at a 2% daily loss. Five consecutive net losing trades lock new entries across days. A flat result leaves the streak unchanged. A gap through a stop can exceed the sizing estimate and the daily threshold. Each arm publishes balances, fees, funding, realized history, open exposure, outcome counts and per-market decision reasons. Journal history is filterable by arm.

## Activation

The V2 container image defaults to `RC3F_DELTA_ARMS=1`, which initializes the two additive books on RC3F-1 and permits new intents. Set `RC3F_DELTA_ARMS=0` at runtime to pause new entries while the same code continues to track existing liabilities. Use a persistent volume with one worker. Keep the new code in place while C/D hold exposure. A rollback to the earlier image would suspend C/D liability tracking. Verify warm-up, resource use, A/B checkpoint preservation, paper-only status and journal integrity after deployment.
