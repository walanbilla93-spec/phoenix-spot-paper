## 3 Structural pivots and directional regimes

**U:** A strict 3-left/3-right swing high exceeds the highs of all three candles on either side; a swing low is below all six neighboring lows. Equal-price ties in those neighborhoods do not qualify. This applies to 5m, 15m, 1H, 4H and Daily. A pivot is usable only after its third right-hand candle closes. Store both its original candle time and its confirmation/availability time. Never make it usable at its plotted historical origin.

**U:** Build 5m, 4H and Daily structural sequences in confirmation order with alternating highs and lows. Consecutive same-type candidates retain the more extreme confirmed pivot: higher high or lower low. Equal same-type prices retain the earlier confirmed pivot. The terminal same-type candidate remains provisional until an opposite-type pivot is accepted; that preceding pivot then freezes. A frozen pivot or historical decision cannot be replaced retrospectively.

**U:** Simultaneous 4H and Daily confirmations are ordered by original pivot-candle timestamp, oldest first. Opposite-type pivots sharing that original timestamp are both excluded from structural-sequence construction. Never invent high-before-low or low-before-high chronology. Keep the exclusions in the journal. **D03:** Apply the same tie-exclusion convention to 5m structural sequencing. Raw confirmed 15m/1H pivots remain the liquidity population; structural alternation must not silently discard otherwise eligible liquidity pivots. A raw high/low pair on the same candle may identify separate liquidity prices, but cannot establish an invented directional sequence.

**U:** A bullish directional sequence requires three progressively higher confirmed swing highs and three progressively higher confirmed swing lows, giving two successive higher-high and higher-low steps. A bearish sequence requires three progressively lower highs and three progressively lower lows. All required points belong to the alternating sequence. A single break or developing pivot cannot establish a regime. No EMA confirmation is required.

**D04 — Completion and transition mechanics:** Daily and 4H regime formation use the latest three frozen highs and latest three frozen lows in their own alternating chains. Do not search back for a convenient older subsequence. Once established, a regime persists until protected-anchor invalidation or a complete fresh opposite sequence. Lack of a new qualifying pattern alone does not erase it. Governing legs may have a confirmed but terminal-provisional endpoint; their anchor must be frozen. Exact timeframe maturity and leg association are specified as RC2 proposed defaults in §3.1 and §9.1.

**U:** In bullish 4H or Daily structure protect the HL anchoring the latest completed HL→HH governing directional leg. In bearish structure protect the LH anchoring the latest LH→LL leg. Update only after a new governing leg is fully confirmed. A completed candle on that regime's own timeframe closing strictly beyond the protected anchor against the regime causes **neutral transition**. A wick breach alone and an exact close at the anchor do not. Neutral is not automatic reversal. A complete new two-step sequence is required to establish the next regime, including a return to the prior direction.

**D05 — Avoid immediate recycling:** At an invalidating close first test the protected level that was in force immediately before that close. Subsequent regime establishment must complete after the transition using at least one newly frozen structural point since that transition; the new protected anchor must survive the current close. ## 6 Context POI construction and lifecycle

The middle candle must point in the FVG direction, have body ≥1.5×ATR(14) on its own timeframe and body/range ≥65%. No extra minimum-gap ATR threshold is inherited from an older script.

**U — Context FVG invalidation:** Long-side FVG invalidates when price touches or falls below its original midpoint; short-side FVG when price touches or rises above it. A wick is sufficient; no close is needed. Shallow mitigation is allowed subject to the two-visit maximum. Once invalid, it cannot support a new setup. Do not erase earlier eligibility decisions or frozen trade parameters.

**U — Context OB:** Bullish origin is the last bearish candle before the bullish displacement leg; bearish origin is the last bullish candle before the bearish leg. Require own-timeframe directional displacement meeting 1.5 ATR and 65%, and a completed close through a previously confirmed structural swing in that direction. The broken swing must be known before the break. Qualifying displacement must occur within the next five candles after origin, and the structural break must occur after origin and within that same five-candle window, on the displacement candle or later. No extended or restarted deadline and no backdated eligibility.

**U:** Intervening candles before displacement may continue in the intended direction. An opposite-colored candle interrupts that leg and disqualifies that original candidate. A doji means open=close, is neutral, and neither advances nor interrupts the leg; every doji consumes a deadline candle. Bullish OB boundaries are origin low to origin open; bearish boundaries are origin open to origin high. Boundaries never expand or shrink. An exact far-wick touch does not invalidate; a strict wick breach beyond origin low for bullish OB or origin high for bearish OB does. Partial mitigation is allowed while the far boundary holds.

**D09 — OB association:** The OB structural-break reference is the latest unbroken, frozen opposing structural pivot on the OB's own timeframe, available before the break candle opens. These association details complete the approved concept without changing the five-candle limit.

**U — Visits:** A visit begins with strict wick penetration inside the confirmed zone. An exact touch of its near boundary does not count, and a close inside is unnecessary. Continuous interaction is one visit, however many candles it lasts. A subsequent visit requires two consecutive completed candles on the POI's own timeframe entirely outside on the approach side, then fresh penetration. For a bullish POI the approach side is above the zone; for a bearish POI it is below. Maximum two distinct visits; visit three makes it ineligible. FVG midpoint invalidation overrides visit counting.

**D10 — Visit timing:** Entirely outside means bullish candle low > zone high or bearish candle high < zone low; equality breaks separation. ### 9.1 Swept-leg association — R2D08 continued

**R2D08 — RC2 proposed default for signoff:** At reclaim use the §3.1 latest-three-frozen-highs/latest-three-frozen-lows sequence. For a long it must be strictly bearish.
