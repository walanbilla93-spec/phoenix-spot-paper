How Phoenix tracks prices and buys / sells

Phoenix waits for a signal; it does not place resting exchange orders in advance. New cycles require a current completed minute to close at or above its open and the previous close, followed by a later fresh execution check. The new entry cap is 0.3% above that signal close; it does not depend on a previous cycle or the prior 24-hour low. DCA and TP use their existing completed-minute rules; ATR uses completed hours. After a signal, a later observation fetches a fresh spot order book and models an immediate fill: buys use sellers' asking prices; sells use buyers' bids. Trigger price, last-traded price and fill price can differ.

DCA requires the ATR / 1% drop threshold followed by a 1.5% rebound from a previously observed trough. Every modeled DCA fill must be below the previous purchase and lower the fee-inclusive average. TP arms 1.6% above average cost and waits for a later 0.2% pullback from the tracked peak. A signal alone never books profit. Freshness, depth, price caps, fees and lot rules can cause a partial fill or rejection. Only the modeled filled quantity changes the paper account.
