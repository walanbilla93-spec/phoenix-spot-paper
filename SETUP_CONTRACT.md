# Executable research contract R1

The source videos leave discretionary definitions unresolved. We now explicitly freeze a mechanical research hypothesis in `research_policy.json`; no claim is made that it reproduces the trader. Changing its numerical rules requires a distinct policy version and ledger. No live trading is enabled.

The primary method keeps every requested confluence: a confirmed H1 wave-1/wave-2 pivot hypothesis and directional SMC structure; a pre-existing 5m OB/FVG; a failed nearer POI and predeclared unconsumed liquidity pivot; a subsequent sweep/reclaim; a fresh displacement close through an unbroken pre-touch structure reference; a complete prior UTC-day value profile; and directional delta plus three adjacent diagonal footprint imbalances. Causal availability timestamps, frozen anchors, source bars and exact trigger levels accompany decisions. Failed early context is rejected before collecting unnecessary raw detail.

The policy specifies the exact thresholds, timeframes, profile expansion, stop, three ladder fractions, total risk, targets, expiry, latency, costs and size model. Model fees/slippage/funding reserve are research assumptions, not verified account charges or exchange fills. The strategy uses all public trade volume consistently, including any block/RPI executions; RPI liquidity is not assumed executable in the ordinary L1 book.

Every qualified campaign waits for a strictly later eligible quote. Additions require independently later confirmation and unchanged risk. Adds cease after profit-taking. Targets change prospectively only after fills; the stop never widens. Crossing a stop triggers a later executable quote, including adverse gaps. Partial exits do not count as completed wins. Censored and revoked campaigns are excluded from the closed denominator. A late correction affecting qualification or outcome evidence can retract an already closed result.

SQLite transactions preserve the event/position relationship; restart censors any unresolved open position. A SHA256 chain covers decisions and outcomes. Exports include a consistent bounded database snapshot and the exact policy. Active trade quotes continue beyond the old ten-minute raw window. Compact exports retain reconciled 5m bars, frozen arrival quotes and frozen profiles for replay; full required archive parts are necessary to reproduce the whole path.

Prior-session profile availability, instrument metadata, H1 context and validated signal bars are visible per coin. An incomplete previous UTC session blocks entries. Future data and retrospective relabelling cannot satisfy an earlier decision. Old capture without exact footprints remains insufficient for this model and is not upgraded to a profitable backtest.

No Phoenix/Orayan settings, funds or existing trade histories are changed. No source-history deletion occurs in this patch. Captures previously cleared by the owner remain cleared.
